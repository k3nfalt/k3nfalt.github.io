Parameter-Free Composite Convex Minimization

We consider minimizing F=f+ψ over a convex set Q in a real Hilbert space E, where f:E→R is convex and continuously differentiable and ψ:E→R is convex on Q. The function f is (L0,L1)-smooth with hidden constants L0>0 and L1≥0. A given point x0∈Q has bounded distance and objective gap to a minimizer x⋆, whose smooth gradient is bounded. The oracle returns exact gradients and constrained proximal minimizers. For ε>0, the goal is x¯∈Q with F(x¯)−F(x⋆)≤ε almost surely under P, the law of the algorithm’s randomness.

Write F(x)=f(x)+ψ(x). The optimization problem is minx∈QF(x). The norm ∥⋅∥ is induced by the inner product ⟨⋅,⋅⟩ on E. The algorithm receives only E, x0, and a scalar h0>0; the parameters L0>0, L1≥0, R>0, Δ>0, G≥0, and ε>0 may enter only the oracle-call budget.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (Convexity). The set Q⊆E is convex, f is convex on E, and ψ is convex on Q. Thus, for t∈[0,1], f((1−t)x+ty)≤(1−t)f(x)+tf(y),x,y∈E,ψ((1−t)x+ty)≤(1−t)ψ(x)+tψ(y),x,y∈Q.

Assumption 2 (Generalized smoothness). The function f is Fréchet differentiable everywhere on E, and ∇f:E→E is continuous. For every x,y∈E and M∈R, the segment bound ∥∇f(x+t(y−x))∥≤Mfor every t∈[0,1] implies ∥∇f(x)−∇f(y)∥≤(L0+L1M)∥x−y∥.

Assumption 3 (Exact proximal minimization). There is a map P:R×E×E→E such that, for every a>0 and z,v∈E, P(a,z,v)∈argminu∈Q{∥u−z∥22a+⟨v,u−z⟩+ψ(u)}.

Assumption 4 (Initial point and minimizer). The given point satisfies x0∈Q. There exists x⋆∈Q such that F(x⋆)≤F(x)for every x∈Q,∥x0−x⋆∥≤R,F(x0)−F(x⋆)≤Δ,∥∇f(x⋆)∥≤G.

The oracle returns ∇f(x) at x. A proximal query (a,z,v) returns P(a,z,v). Gradient queries may use any x∈E, and proximal queries may use any a∈R and z,v∈E; the proximal minimization condition applies when a>0. Both query types count toward a single total oracle-call budget.

After the allotted calls, the output must satisfy x¯∈Q,F(x¯)−F(x⋆)≤εP-a.s. The minimizer x⋆ is fixed independently of the algorithm’s randomness.

Minimize F=f+ψ over convex Q in a real Hilbert space. The function f is convex with continuous gradient and satisfies ∥∇f(x)−∇f(y)∥≤(L0+L1M)∥x−y∥ whenever ∥∇f(z)∥≤M on [x,y]; ψ is finite-valued and convex on Q. Exact gradients and minimizers of ψ(u)+⟨v,u−z⟩+∥u−z∥2/(2a) over u∈Q are available for every a>0,z,v. There is a minimizer x⋆∈Q with x0∈Q, ∥x0−x⋆∥≤R, F(x0)−F(x⋆)≤Δ, and ∥∇f(x⋆)∥≤G. Here L0,R,Δ,h0,ε>0 and L1,G≥0. Target: deterministic x^∈Q with F(x^)−F(x⋆)≤ε. The constructed method receives only (x0,h0); its stopping budget depends on all setup parameters.
