Nonconvex Optimization with Auxiliary Similarity

We consider minimizing a possibly nonconvex function f:E→R on a finite-dimensional real inner product space E, with a differentiable auxiliary function h:E→R. The objective is twice continuously differentiable with Lipschitz Hessian; both the auxiliary gradient and the gradient difference ∇f−∇h are Lipschitz. A given point x0∈E has bounded objective gap above the infimum. Two exact oracles return (f(x),∇f(x)) and (h(x),∇h(x)) at a query x∈E. For ε>0, the goal is an output x¯∈E satisfying ∥∇f(x¯)∥≤ε almost surely under P, the law of the algorithm’s randomness.

The optimization problem is infx∈Ef(x). The norm ∥⋅∥ is induced by the inner product on E, and ∥⋅∥op denotes the induced operator norm. The parameters satisfy L≥0,δ>0,ρ>0,Δ≥0,ε>0. The algorithm receives E, x0, L, δ, ρ, Δ, and ε.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (Hessian regularity). The objective f is twice continuously Fréchet differentiable on E. Its Hessian, viewed as a linear operator from E to E, satisfies ∥∇2f(x)−∇2f(y)∥op≤ρ∥x−y∥,x,y∈E.

Assumption 2 (Auxiliary smoothness and similarity). The auxiliary function h is Fréchet differentiable on E. For every x,y∈E, ∥∇h(x)−∇h(y)∥≤L∥x−y∥,∥(∇f(x)−∇h(x))−(∇f(y)−∇h(y))∥≤δ∥x−y∥.

Assumption 3 (Initial objective gap). The given point x0 satisfies f(x0)−f(x)≤Δ,x∈E.

Both oracles accept queries at any point of E, and their calls are counted separately. After the allotted calls, the required accuracy is ∥∇f(x¯)∥≤εP-a.s.

Results

  • Auxiliary Similarity Acceleration · Lean proof

On a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, f∈C2, h is differentiable, ∇2f is ρ-Lipschitz, ∇h is L-Lipschitz, and ∇(f−h) is δ-Lipschitz. Assume f(x0)−f(x)≤Δ for every x, L,Δ≥0, and δ,ρ,ε>0. Exact oracles return (f,∇f) and (h,∇h). The target is a returned point x^ with ∥∇f(x^)∥≤ε almost surely; (1) is deterministic.
