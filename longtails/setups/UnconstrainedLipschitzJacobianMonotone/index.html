Monotone Equations with Lipschitz Jacobians

We consider finding a zero of a differentiable monotone map F:E→E on a finite-dimensional real inner product space E. Its Jacobian F′ is L-Lipschitz in operator norm, where L≥0, and a given point x0∈E lies within distance D>0 of a zero x⋆. Two exact oracles return F(x) and F′(x), respectively, at x∈E. For a tolerance ε>0, the goal is an output x¯∈E satisfying ∥F(x¯)∥≤ε almost surely under P, the law of the algorithm’s randomness.

The equation to be solved is F(x)=0,x∈E. Write ⟨⋅,⋅⟩ for the inner product and ∥⋅∥ for its induced norm. The Jacobian F′(x):E→E is the Fréchet derivative, and ∥⋅∥op denotes the induced operator norm. The algorithm receives E, x0, L, D, ε, and an integer parameter m≥1.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (Monotonicity). For every x,y∈E, ⟨F(x)−F(y),x−y⟩≥0.

Assumption 2 (Lipschitz Jacobian). The map F is Fréchet differentiable everywhere on E, and ∥F′(x)−F′(y)∥op≤L∥x−y∥,x,y∈E.

Assumption 3 (Initial distance to a zero). There exists x⋆∈E such that F(x⋆)=0,∥x0−x⋆∥≤D.

Both oracles accept queries at any point of E. Calls to the map oracle and the Jacobian oracle are counted separately. After the allotted calls, the output must satisfy the residual criterion ∥F(x¯)∥≤εP-a.s.

Results

  • Lazy Anchored Extra Proximal · Lean proof

On a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, F is differentiable and monotone, with globally L-Lipschitz Jacobian and a zero x⋆ satisfying ∥x0−x⋆∥≤D. Exact value and Jacobian oracles are available; L≥0, D,ε>0, and integer m≥1. The target is ∥F(z)∥≤ε (deterministically for our construction).
