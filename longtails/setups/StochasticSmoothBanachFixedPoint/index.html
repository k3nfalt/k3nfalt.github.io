Stochastic Banach Fixed Points

We consider finding a fixed point of a nonexpansive map T:E→E on a finite-dimensional real Banach space E with a smooth squared norm controlled by C≥1. A given point x0∈E is within distance R≥0 of a fixed point. At x∈E, the oracle returns an unbiased estimate of T(x) using a fresh independent sample, with expected squared error at most σ2, where σ≥0. For ε>0, the goal is a measurable output x¯∈E with EP∥T(x¯)−x¯∥2≤ε2, where P is the joint law of sampling and independent algorithm randomness.

The fixed-point problem is to find x∈E such that T(x)=x. Write ∥⋅∥ for the norm on E and E∗ for its continuous real dual. Operator norms are the induced operator norms. The algorithm receives E, the measurable sample space Ω, x0, C, R, σ, and ε; it accesses T through the stochastic oracle.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (Smooth squared norm). There exists a continuous map J:E→E∗ such that, for every x,y∈E, ∥J(x)∥op≤∥x∥,∥x+y∥2≤∥x∥2+2J(x)[y]+C∥y∥2.

Assumption 2 (Nonexpansiveness and initial distance). The map T satisfies ∥T(x)−T(y)∥≤∥x−y∥,x,y∈E. There exists x⋆∈E such that T(x⋆)=x⋆,∥x0−x⋆∥≤R.

Assumption 3 (Stochastic oracle). There exist a probability measure ν on Ω and a jointly measurable map g:E×Ω→E. For every x∈E, the map g(x,⋅) is Bochner integrable under ν, the squared error ∥g(x,⋅)−T(x)∥2 is integrable, and Eξ∼ν[g(x,ξ)]=T(x),Eξ∼ν[∥g(x,ξ)−T(x)∥2]≤σ2. At call k≥0, a query x∈E receives the reply g(x,ξk). The measurable random variables (ξk)k≥0 are mutually independent with common law ν, and the entire sample sequence is independent of the algorithm’s randomness.

Queries may be made at any point of E. After the allotted oracle calls, the output x¯ must be measurable, its squared fixed-point residual must be integrable under P, and it must satisfy EP[∥T(x¯)−x¯∥2]≤ε2.

Results

  • Stochastic Smooth Banach Fixed Point · Lean proof

A finite-dimensional real Banach space E with its Borel measurable structure; C≥1, R,σ≥0, ε>0. There is a continuous J:E→E∗ with ∥J(x)∥≤∥x∥ and ∥x+y∥2≤∥x∥2+2J(x)[y]+C∥y∥2. The nonexpansive map T:E→E has Tx⋆=x⋆ and ∥x0−x⋆∥≤R. A jointly measurable oracle g satisfies ∫g(x,ξ)dp(ξ)=Tx and ∫∥g(x,ξ)−Tx∥2dp(ξ)≤σ2, with these integrands integrable. Samples are IID with marginal probability measure p under probability measure P. Target: measurable output Z with integrable squared residual and ∫∥TZ−Z∥2dP≤ε2. The construction uses R,C,σ,ε; the setup imposes no algorithm restriction.

Abbr: R: initial fixed-point distance bound; σ2: oracle variance bound; C: squared-norm smoothness (C=p−1 in ℓp, 2≤p<∞); ℓ=1+log⁡(1+R/ε); D: invariant-domain diameter; ℓδ=1+log⁡(1+D/(εδ)); HP: residual at most ε with probability at least 1−δ, 0<δ<1; RMS: root mean square. Costs count individual oracle evaluations, including batches and paired queries. polylog denotes logarithmic factors left unspecified in the cited bounds. The subscript κ,log⁡(1/δ) retains unspecified smoothability and logarithmic-confidence dependence: κ=Ba2 for an equivalent squared-norm smoothness constant B and norm distortion a (native smooth case: κ=C).
