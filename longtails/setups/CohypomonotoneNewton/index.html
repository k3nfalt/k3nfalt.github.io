Cohypomonotone Root Finding

We consider finding a zero of a differentiable map F:E→E on a finite-dimensional real inner product space E. The map is ρ-cohypomonotone, where ρ≥0, and its derivative J(x)=DF(x) is H-Lipschitz in operator norm, where H>0. A given starting point x0∈E lies within distance R>0 of a zero x⋆ of F. The oracle returns (F(x),J(x)) at x. For a tolerance ε>0, the goal is an output x¯∈E satisfying ∥F(x¯)∥≤ε almost surely under P, the law of the algorithm’s randomness.

The root-finding problem is to find x∈E such that F(x)=0. Write ⟨⋅,⋅⟩ for the inner product and ∥⋅∥ for its induced norm; ∥⋅∥op denotes the induced operator norm for linear maps from E to E. The algorithm receives E, H, ρ, R, ε, and x0.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (Lipschitz derivative). The map F is Fréchet differentiable everywhere on E, with derivative J(x):E→E, and ∥J(x)−J(y)∥op≤H∥x−y∥,x,y∈E.

Assumption 2 (Cohypomonotonicity). For every x,y∈E, ⟨F(x)−F(y),x−y⟩≥−ρ∥F(x)−F(y)∥2.

Assumption 3 (Initial distance to a zero). There exists x⋆∈E such that F(x⋆)=0,∥x0−x⋆∥≤R.

Queries may be made at any point of E, and both components of the oracle reply are exact. After the allotted oracle calls, the output must satisfy the residual criterion ∥F(x¯)∥≤εP-a.s.

  • Cohypomonotone Newton Acceleration · Lean proof

On a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, F is differentiable, ∥DF(x)−DF(y)∥≤H∥x−y∥, and ⟨F(x)−F(y),x−y⟩≥−ρ∥F(x)−F(y)∥2. A root x⋆ satisfies ∥x0−x⋆∥≤R, with H,R,ε>0 and ρ≥0. Exact paired (F,DF) queries are available. The target is ∥F(xout)∥≤ε almost surely; (1) holds for every seed.
