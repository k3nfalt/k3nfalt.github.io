Generalized Lipschitz Minty Root Finding

We seek a zero of a continuous operator F:Rd→Rd in Euclidean dimension d≥0. On each segment, F is Lipschitz with constant L0+L1M, where L0>0, L1≥0, and M is the maximum of ∥F∥ on that segment. A given starting point x0 lies within distance R≥0 of a zero x⋆ satisfying ⟨F(x),x−x⋆⟩≥0 for every x∈Rd. The oracle returns F(x) at x. For ε>0, the goal is an output x¯∈Rd with E∥F(x¯)∥≤ε, where the expectation is over the algorithm’s randomness.

In terms of the Euclidean residual norm, the problem is minx∈Rd∥F(x)∥. The algorithm receives d∈{0,1,2,…}, L0, L1, R, ε, and x0∈Rd, and may query any point in Rd.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (Generalized Lipschitz continuity). The operator F is continuous. For every x,y∈Rd, let Mx,y denote the maximum operator value in norm along the segment from x to y. Then Mx,y=maxt∈[0,1]∥F(x+t(y−x))∥,∥F(x)−F(y)∥≤(L0+L1Mx,y)∥x−y∥.

Assumption 2 (Minty zero and initial distance). There exists x⋆∈Rd such that F(x⋆)=0, ∥x0−x⋆∥≤R, and ⟨F(x),x−x⋆⟩≥0for every x∈Rd.

After the allotted oracle calls, the residual norm ∥F(x¯)∥ must be integrable under the law P of the algorithm’s randomness and satisfy EP[∥F(x¯)∥]≤ε.

Proved result

2⋅(1+⌈32⋅(R⋅(L0ε+L1))2⌉+)
