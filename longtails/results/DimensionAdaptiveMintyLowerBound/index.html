Finite-Dimensional Weak Minty Root Finding

We seek an approximate zero of a Lipschitz operator F:Rd→Rd in dimension d≥1. A given starting point x0∈Rd lies within a prescribed radius of a zero x⋆ at which F satisfies the weak Minty condition. The oracle returns F(x) at any query point x∈Rd. For a tolerance ε>0, the goal is an output x¯∈Rd with integrable Euclidean residual norm satisfying E[∥F(x¯)∥]≤ε, where the expectation is over the algorithm’s randomness.

The underlying equation is F(x)=0,x∈Rd. We use the Euclidean inner product and its induced norm. The algorithm receives d, x0, the Lipschitz constant L, the weak Minty parameter ρ, the radius R, and the tolerance ε, with d∈{1,2,…},L>0,0≤ρ≤120L,0<ε<LR.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (Lipschitz continuity). For all x,y∈Rd, ∥F(x)−F(y)∥≤L∥x−y∥.

Assumption 2 (Weak Minty condition at a nearby zero). There exists x⋆∈Rd with F(x⋆)=0 and ∥x⋆−x0∥≤R such that, for every x∈Rd, ⟨F(x),x−x⋆⟩≥−ρ∥F(x)∥2.

After the allotted oracle calls, the output x¯ must satisfy ∥F(x¯)∥∈L1(P),EP[∥F(x¯)∥]≤ε, where P is the law of the algorithm’s randomness.

Proved result
