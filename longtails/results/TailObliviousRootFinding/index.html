Tail-Oblivious Monotone Root Finding

We seek a zero of a monotone, L-Lipschitz operator F:E→E on a finite-dimensional real inner product space E, where L>0. A given point x0∈E lies within distance R≥0 of a zero x⋆. The oracle returns an unbiased random vector estimating F(x) at each query x, using independent, identically distributed samples, with centered α-moment at most σα, where 1<α≤2 and σ≥0. The algorithm knows only E, x0, and L. For ε>0, the goal is a measurable output x¯∈E satisfying EP∥F(x¯)∥α≤εα, where P is the joint law of independent algorithm and oracle randomness.

The root-finding problem is find x∈E such that F(x)=0. Write ⟨⋅,⋅⟩ for the inner product and ∥⋅∥ for its induced norm. The parameters α, σ, R, and ε may enter the oracle-call budget, but are not algorithm inputs.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (Monotonicity and Lipschitz continuity). For all x,y∈E, ⟨F(x)−F(y),x−y⟩≥0,∥F(x)−F(y)∥≤L∥x−y∥.

Assumption 2 (Nearby zero). There exists x⋆∈E such that F(x⋆)=0,∥x0−x⋆∥≤R.

Assumption 3 (Heavy-tailed stochastic oracle). Let Ω be a measurable sample space and ν a probability measure on Ω. There is a jointly measurable map g:E×Ω→E. For every x∈E, the map g(x,⋅) is Bochner integrable and ∥g(x,⋅)−F(x)∥α is integrable under ν, with Eξ∼ν[g(x,ξ)]=F(x),Eξ∼ν[∥g(x,ξ)−F(x)∥α]≤σα. At call k≥0, a query xk∈E receives g(xk,ξk), where the measurable sample variables (ξk)k≥0 are mutually independent and each has law ν. The sample sequence is independent of the algorithm’s randomness.

Queries may be made at any point of E. After the allotted oracle calls, the output x¯ must be measurable, its residual moment ∥F(x¯)∥α must be integrable under P, and it must satisfy EP[∥F(x¯)∥α]≤εα.
