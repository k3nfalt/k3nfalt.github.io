Similar Finite-Sum Quasar Minimization

We consider minimizing F(x)=n−1∑i=1nfi(x) on a real Hilbert space E, where n≥1 and the functions fi:E→R are differentiable. The potentially nonconvex objective has a Lipschitz gradient, satisfies a quadratic lower model, and obeys strong quasar-convexity and quadratic growth relative to a global minimizer x⋆. Component-gradient deviations from the mean are Lipschitz with a restricted similarity constant. A given point x0∈E has bounded objective gap. The oracle returns ∇fi(x) at a queried pair (i,x). For ε>0, the goal is an output x¯∈E with F(x¯)−F(x⋆)≤ε almost surely under the algorithm’s randomness.

The optimization problem is minx∈EF(x). The norm ∥⋅∥ is induced by the inner product ⟨⋅,⋅⟩ on E. The parameters satisfy 0<μ≤L,0<γ≤1,δ≥0,384δ2≤γ3μ2,Δ≥0,ε>0. The algorithm receives E, n, L, μ, γ, δ, Δ, ε, and x0.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (Smoothness and component similarity). Each fi is Fréchet differentiable everywhere on E. For every x,y∈E and i∈{1,…,n}, ∥∇F(x)−∇F(y)∥≤L∥x−y∥,∥(∇fi(x)−∇F(x))−(∇fi(y)−∇F(y))∥≤δ∥x−y∥.
