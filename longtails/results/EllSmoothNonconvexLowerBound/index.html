High-Dimensional ℓ-Smooth Nonconvex Optimization

We seek an approximate stationary point of a possibly nonconvex function f:Rd→R in high dimension d. Its Hessian exists everywhere and has operator norm bounded by ℓ(∥∇f(x)∥) at each point x, where ℓ:R→R is positive and nondecreasing on [0,∞) with subquadratic growth. A given starting point x0∈Rd has objective value within Δ≥0 of the infimum. The oracle returns (f(x),∇f(x)) at x. For a tolerance ε>0, the goal is a measurable output x¯∈Rd with E[∥∇f(x¯)∥]≤ε, where the expectation is over the algorithm’s randomness.

The underlying optimization problem is infx∈Rdf(x). We use the Euclidean norm ∥⋅∥ and its induced operator norm ∥⋅∥op. The algorithm receives d, ℓ, the growth exponent ρ∈[0,2), Δ, ε, and x0.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (Smoothness profile). For 0≤u≤v, the profile satisfies 0<ℓ(u)≤ℓ(v). Its growth on positive arguments satisfies ℓ(v)vρ≤ℓ(u)uρ,0<u≤v.

Assumption 2 (Curvature and initial gap). The function f and its gradient are Fréchet differentiable everywhere. For every x∈Rd, ∥∇2f(x)∥op≤ℓ(∥∇f(x)∥),f(x0)−f(x)≤Δ.

Assumption 3 (Accuracy and dimension regime). Let s=(2−ρ)Δℓ(ε)/(227ε2). The tolerance and the dimension d∈N satisfy s≥1,d≥256(⌊s⌋+1)6(ℓ(ε)ℓ(0))4.

Queries may be made at any point of Rd. After the allotted oracle calls, the output x¯ must be measurable, and its gradient norm must be integrable and satisfy E[∥∇f(x¯)∥]≤ε.
