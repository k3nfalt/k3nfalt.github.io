Stationarity in ℓ-Smooth Nonconvex Minimization

We seek an approximately stationary point of a possibly nonconvex, twice differentiable function f:Rd→R in Euclidean dimension d≥0. The Hessian operator norm is bounded by ℓ(∥∇f(x)∥) at each point x, where ℓ:R→R is nondecreasing on nonnegative arguments, positive on positive arguments, and grows at most quadratically under scaling. The given starting point x0∈Rd has objective gap at most Δ≥0 above the infimum. The oracle returns (f(x),∇f(x)) at x. For tolerance ε>0, the goal is a measurable output x¯∈Rd satisfying E[∥∇f(x¯)∥]≤ε over the algorithm’s randomness.

The underlying optimization problem is infx∈Rdf(x). The algorithm receives the integer dimension d, the function ℓ, its growth exponent ρ∈[0,2], the gap bound Δ, the tolerance ε, and the starting point x0. We use the Euclidean norm ∥⋅∥ and its induced operator norm ∥⋅∥op.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (Growth of the smoothness function). The function ℓ satisfies ℓ(u)≤ℓ(v)(0≤u≤v),ℓ(v)uρ≤ℓ(u)vρ(0<u≤v),ℓ(u)>0(u>0).

Assumption 2 (Gradient-dependent curvature). The function f and its gradient are Fréchet differentiable throughout Rd, and the Hessian satisfies ∥∇2f(x)∥op≤ℓ(∥∇f(x)∥),x∈Rd.

Assumption 3 (Initial objective gap). The starting point satisfies f(x0)−f(x)≤Δ,x∈Rd.

Queries may be made anywhere in Rd. After the allotted oracle calls, the output x¯ must be measurable and its gradient norm integrable under P, the law of the algorithm’s randomness. The accuracy criterion is EP[∥∇f(x¯)∥]≤ε.

Proved result

⌊16⋅Δ⋅ℓ(ε)ε2⌋++1
