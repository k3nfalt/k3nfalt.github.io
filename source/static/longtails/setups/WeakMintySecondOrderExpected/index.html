Second-Order Weak Minty Root Finding

We seek a zero of a Lipschitz map F:E→E on a finite-dimensional real inner product space E. Its Jacobian J is Lipschitz, and a given starting point x0∈E lies within distance R≥0 of a zero x⋆ satisfying the weak Minty condition. At any x∈E, the oracle returns either F(x) or J(x), as requested. For a tolerance ε>0, the goal is an output x¯∈E with integrable residual norm satisfying E[∥F(x¯)∥]≤ε, where the expectation is over the algorithm’s randomness.

The root-finding problem is F(x)=0,x∈E. The algorithm receives E, the Lipschitz constants L1,L2>0 for F and J, respectively, the weak Minty parameter 0≤ρ≤1/L1, the tolerance ε, and the starting point x0. The radius R may enter only the oracle-call budget.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (Operator and Jacobian regularity). The map F is Fréchet differentiable at every x∈E, with derivative J(x):E→E. For all x,y∈E, ∥F(x)−F(y)∥≤L1∥x−y∥,∥J(x)−J(y)∥op≤L2∥x−y∥, where ∥⋅∥op is the operator norm induced by the inner product norm on E.

Assumption 2 (Weak Minty condition at a nearby zero). There exists x⋆∈E with F(x⋆)=0 and ∥x0−x⋆∥≤R such that, for every x∈E, ⟨F(x),x−x⋆⟩≥−ρ2∥F(x)∥2.

Each call requests either an operator value or a full Jacobian, and both query types count toward a single budget. After the allotted calls, the output must satisfy ∥F(x¯)∥∈L1(P),EP[∥F(x¯)∥]≤ε, where P is the law of the algorithm’s randomness.

Results

  • Second Order Weak Minty Upper Bound · Lean proof

On a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, F is L1-Lipschitz and its Jacobian is L2-Lipschitz, with L1,L2>0. A root x⋆ satisfies ∥x0−x⋆∥≤R and ⟨F(x),x−x⋆⟩≥−(ρ/2)∥F(x)∥2 for all x, with ρ≥0 and ρL1≤1. Queries return exact values or full Jacobians. The target is an integrable residual with ∫∥F(xout(ω))∥dP(ω)≤ε, where P is the execution law and ε>0.
