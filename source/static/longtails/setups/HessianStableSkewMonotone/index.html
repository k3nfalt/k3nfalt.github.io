Hessian-Stable Skew Monotone Equations

We seek a zero of a differentiable map F:E→E on a finite-dimensional real inner product space E. Its Jacobian has a constant skew-adjoint part and a positive semidefinite symmetric part with exponential stability parameter M>0. A given point x0∈E lies within distance D>0 of a zero x⋆, around which the Jacobian satisfies a local bound with hidden constant Λ≥0. Two exact oracles return F(x) and its Jacobian J(x)=DF(x). For ε>0, the goal is an output x¯∈E with ∥F(x¯)∥≤ε almost surely under P, the law of the algorithm’s randomness.

The underlying equation is F(x)=0,x∈E. Write ⟨⋅,⋅⟩ for the inner product and ∥⋅∥ for its induced norm; ∥⋅∥op denotes the induced operator norm. The algorithm receives E, x0, M, D, and ε; Λ may enter the oracle-call budgets but is not an algorithm input.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (Skew decomposition and multiplicative stability). The map F is Fréchet differentiable everywhere on E. There exists a fixed linear operator S:E→E such that, with H(x)=J(x)−S, for every x,y,u,v∈E, ⟨Sv,v⟩=0,⟨H(x)u,v⟩=⟨u,H(x)v⟩,⟨H(x)v,v⟩≥0,⟨H(y)v,v⟩≤eM∥y−x∥⟨H(x)v,v⟩.

Assumption 2 (Nearby zero and local Jacobian bound). There exists x⋆∈E such that F(x⋆)=0,∥x0−x⋆∥≤D. Around this same zero, the full Jacobian satisfies ∥J(x)∥op≤Λwhenever∥x−x⋆∥≤3D+14M.

Queries to either oracle may be made at any point of E. A value-oracle call returns F(x), and a Jacobian-oracle call returns the full linear operator J(x):E→E; the two types of calls are counted separately. After the allotted calls, the required residual accuracy is ∥F(x¯)∥≤εP-a.s.

Results

  • Hessian Stable Skew Newton · Lean proof

On a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, F is differentiable with DF(x)=S+H(x), constant skew-adjoint S, and symmetric positive-semidefinite H(x) satisfying H(y)⪯eM∥y−x∥H(x). A root x⋆ satisfies ∥x0−x⋆∥≤D; ∥DF(x)∥≤Λ on B(x⋆,3D+1/(4M)). Here M,D,ε>0 and Λ≥0. Exact F and DF oracles are available. The constructed deterministic output satisfies ∥F(x^)∥≤ε; Λ is needed only for the budget.
