Generalized Smooth Convex Minimization

We consider minimizing a convex function f:Rd→R in dimension d∈N. The function f is (L0,L1)-smooth, meaning its differentiable gradient satisfies ∥∇2f(x)∥op≤L0+L1∥∇f(x)∥ everywhere, with L0>0 and L1≥0. A given point x0∈Rd lies within distance R>0 of a global minimizer x⋆. The oracle returns ∇f(x) at x. For a tolerance ε>0, the goal is an output x¯∈Rd with integrable objective gap and EP[f(x¯)−f(x⋆)]≤ε, where P is the law of the algorithm’s randomness.

The optimization problem is minx∈Rdf(x). We use the Euclidean norm and its induced operator norm. The parameters obey L0>0,L1≥0,R>0,0<ε≤L0R216,d≥⌈17592186044416(1+L1R+L0R2ε)5⌉. The algorithm receives d, L0, L1, x0, R, and ε.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (Convexity). For every x,y∈Rd and t∈[0,1], f((1−t)x+ty)≤(1−t)f(x)+tf(y).

Assumption 2 (Generalized smoothness). The function f is differentiable everywhere, its gradient is Fréchet differentiable everywhere, and ∥∇2f(x)∥op≤L0+L1∥∇f(x)∥,x∈Rd. Here ∇2f(x):Rd→Rd is the derivative of the gradient.

Assumption 3 (Initial distance). There exists x⋆∈Rd such that f(x⋆)≤f(x)for every x∈Rd,∥x0−x⋆∥≤R.

Queries may be made at any point of Rd. After the allotted oracle calls, the output must have an integrable objective gap under P and satisfy EP[f(x¯)−f(x⋆)]≤ε.

Results

  • Full Generalized Smooth Convex Lower Bound · Lean proof

Convex f:Rd→R has a differentiable gradient with ∥∇2f(x)∥≤L0+L1∥∇f(x)∥ and a minimizer x⋆ satisfying ∥x0−x⋆∥≤R. Public parameters satisfy L0,R,ε>0, L1≥0, ε≤L0R2/16, and d≥⌈244(1+L1R+L0R2/ε)5⌉. Arbitrary adaptive randomized algorithms receive exact gradients. The target is integrable expected objective error ∫[f(x^(ω))−f(x⋆)]dP(ω)≤ε, where P is the algorithm’s seed law.
