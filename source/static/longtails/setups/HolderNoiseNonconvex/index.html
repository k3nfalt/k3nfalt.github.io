Nonconvex Optimization with Hölder Noise

We consider minimizing a differentiable, possibly nonconvex function f:E→R on a separable real Hilbert space E. Its gradient is L-Lipschitz, with L>0, and a given point x0∈E satisfies f(x0)−infx∈Ef(x)≤Δ, with Δ≥0. Each oracle call returns unbiased gradients at two queried points using one shared sample, with independent samples across calls. The centered noise has variance at most σ2 and mean-square Hölder increments bounded by M∥x−y∥2ν, where σ,M≥0 and 0<ν≤1. The goal is a measurable output x¯∈E satisfying E∥∇f(x¯)∥≤ε, where ε>0 and expectation includes sampling and algorithm randomness.

The optimization problem is infx∈Ef(x), where ∥⋅∥ is the norm induced by the inner product on E. The algorithm receives E, the measurable sample space Ω, L, M, ν, σ, Δ, ε, and x0.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (Smoothness and initial gap). The function f is Fréchet differentiable everywhere, and for every x,y∈E, ∥∇f(x)−∇f(y)∥≤L∥x−y∥,f(x0)−f(x)≤Δ.

Assumption 2 (Unbiased stochastic gradients). There exist a probability measure p on Ω and a jointly measurable map g:E×Ω→E. For every x∈E, the random vector g(x,ξ) is Bochner integrable and ∥g(x,ξ)−∇f(x)∥2 is integrable under ξ∼p, with Eξ∼p[g(x,ξ)]=∇f(x),Eξ∼p[∥g(x,ξ)−∇f(x)∥2]≤σ2.

Assumption 3 (Hölder noise increments). Define the centered noise by ζ(x,ξ)=g(x,ξ)−∇f(x). For every x,y∈E, the squared increment ∥ζ(x,ξ)−ζ(y,ξ)∥2 is integrable under ξ∼p and satisfies Eξ∼p[∥ζ(x,ξ)−ζ(y,ξ)∥2]≤M∥x−y∥2ν.

At call k, a query (xk,yk)∈E×E receives (g(xk,ξk),g(yk,ξk)). The sample sequence (ξk)k≥0 consists of measurable, mutually independent random variables with common law p. Algorithm randomness is independent of the entire sample sequence. Queries may depend on earlier replies, and each paired reply counts as one oracle call.

After the allotted calls, the output x¯ must be measurable, its gradient norm must be integrable, and EP[∥∇f(x¯)∥]≤ε, where P is the joint law of the sample sequence and the algorithm’s randomness.

Results

  • Holder Noise Minimax Upper · Lean proof

On a separable real Hilbert space with its Borel measurable structure, f is differentiable with L-Lipschitz gradient and f(x0)−f(x)≤Δ for every x. Here L,ε>0, M,σ,Δ≥0, and 0<ν≤1. The jointly measurable oracle g uses IID samples with law p, satisfies ∫g(x,ω)dp(ω)=∇f(x), and, with e(x,ω)=g(x,ω)−∇f(x), satisfies ∫∥e(x,ω)∥2dp(ω)≤σ2 and ∫∥e(x,ω)−e(y,ω)∥2dp(ω)≤M∥x−y∥2ν, with the stated integrands integrable. One call returns gradients at two points using one shared sample. Target: a measurable output with integrable gradient norm and ∫∥∇f(xout(ω))∥dP(ω)≤ε, where P is the sampling law. The setup imposes no algorithm restriction.
