Hessian-Lipschitz Minimization Under Gradient Dominance

We minimize a twice differentiable function f:E→R on a finite-dimensional real inner product space E. Its Hessian is H-Lipschitz for H>0, and f satisfies the Polyak–Łojasiewicz inequality with constant μ>0. The given starting point x0∈E has objective gap at most Δ≥0 relative to a global minimizer x⋆. At any x∈E, the oracle returns (f(x),∇f(x),∇2f(x)). For ε>0, the goal is a measurable output x¯∈E with integrable gap satisfying E[f(x¯)−f(x⋆)]≤ε, where the expectation is over the algorithm’s randomness.

The optimization problem is minx∈Ef(x). The algorithm receives only E, H, and x0. The parameters μ, Δ, and ε may enter only the oracle-call budget. We use the inner product norm on E and the induced operator norm ∥⋅∥op on linear maps from E to E.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (Lipschitz Hessian). The function f and its gradient are Fréchet differentiable everywhere on E. The Hessian satisfies ∥∇2f(x)−∇2f(y)∥op≤H∥x−y∥,x,y∈E.

Assumption 2 (Gradient dominance and initial gap). There exists a global minimizer x⋆∈E such that 2μ(f(x)−f(x⋆))≤∥∇f(x)∥2for every x∈E,f(x0)−f(x⋆)≤Δ.

After the allotted oracle calls, the output must satisfy the expected objective-gap criterion E[f(x¯)−f(x⋆)]≤ε.

  • Hessian Lipschitz PLCubic Newton · Lean proof

On a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, f is twice differentiable with an H-Lipschitz Hessian in operator norm and an attained minimum f⋆. Assume f(x0)−f⋆≤Δ, 2μ(f−f⋆)≤∥∇f∥2 globally, H,μ,ε>0, and Δ≥0. Budgets count exact (f,∇f,∇2f) calls sufficient for an objective gap at most ε (in expectation in the setup, achieved deterministically here).
