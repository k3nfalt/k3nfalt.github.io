Smooth Polyak–Łojasiewicz Minimization

We consider minimizing a differentiable, possibly nonconvex function f:E→R on a real Hilbert space E. Its gradient is L-Lipschitz, and f satisfies the Polyak–Łojasiewicz inequality with parameter 0<μ≤L. A global minimizer x⋆∈E exists, and a given starting point x0∈E satisfies f(x0)−f(x⋆)≤Δ, where Δ≥0. The oracle returns ∇f(x) at x. For ε>0, the goal is an output x¯∈E satisfying f(x¯)−f(x⋆)≤ε almost surely under P, the law of the algorithm’s randomness.

The optimization problem is minx∈Ef(x). The norm ∥⋅∥ is induced by the inner product on E. The algorithm receives only E, L, and x0; the parameters μ, Δ, and ε may enter the oracle-call budget but are not algorithm inputs.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (Smoothness). The function f is Fréchet differentiable everywhere on E, and ∥∇f(x)−∇f(y)∥≤L∥x−y∥,x,y∈E.

Assumption 2 (Polyak–Łojasiewicz inequality and initial gap). There exists x⋆∈E such that f(x⋆)≤f(x)for every x∈E,f(x0)−f(x⋆)≤Δ,2μ(f(x)−f(x⋆))≤∥∇f(x)∥2for every x∈E.

Queries may be made at any point of E. After the allotted oracle calls, the output must satisfy f(x¯)−f(x⋆)≤εP-a.s. The minimizer is fixed independently of the algorithm’s randomness; any global minimizer gives the same objective value.

Results

  • Gd PL · Lean proof

On a real Hilbert space, f has an L-Lipschitz gradient and a global minimizer x⋆, with f(x0)−f(x⋆)≤Δ and 2μ(f(x)−f(x⋆))≤∥∇f(x)∥2 for all x. Here 0<μ≤L, Δ≥0, and ε>0; convexity is not required. The oracle returns exact gradients. Target: f(xK)−f(x⋆)≤ε deterministically. The setup does not restrict the algorithm; (1) constructs GD with step 1/L.

Abbr: GD: gradient descent; PL: Polyak–Łojasiewicz; L: gradient Lipschitz constant; μ: PL constant; Δ: initial objective-gap bound; ε: target gap; K: gradient-call count; log+⁡t=max{0,log⁡t} for t>0, with log+⁡0=0; C: universal positive constant.
