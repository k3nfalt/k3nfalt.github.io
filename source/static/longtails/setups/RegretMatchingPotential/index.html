Regret Matching+ in Potential Games

We consider a two-player game with payoff matrices A,C∈Rm×n, where m,n≥1 are the players’ numbers of actions. Mixed strategies are probability vectors in Rm and Rn. Unilateral payoff differences agree with those of an exact potential taking values in [0,B], where B>0. The oracle returns Ay and C⊤x at a strategy pair (x,y). For ε>0, Regret Matching+ must return probability vectors (x¯,y¯) for which neither player can increase its expected payoff by more than ε through any unilateral pure-action deviation.

Write Δd={z∈Rd:zi≥0 for all i, ∑i=1dzi=1}. The players maximize the respective payoffs x⊤Ay and x⊤Cy over x∈Δm and y∈Δn. The algorithm receives only m, n, and ε; the bound B may enter the oracle-call budget but is not an algorithm input.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (Bounded exact potential). There exists a matrix H∈Rm×n such that, for all i,i′∈{1,…,m} and j,j′∈{1,…,n}, 0≤Hij≤B,Aij−Ai′j=Hij−Hi′j,Cij−Cij′=Hij−Hij′.

Queries may be made at any (x,y)∈Rm×Rn. One oracle call returns both exact payoff vectors ui=∑j=1nAijyj,i=1,…,m,vj=∑i=1mCijxi,j=1,…,n.

The setup is algorithm-specific: we analyze Regret Matching+ with simultaneous updates and retention of the most recent pair passing the deviation test. For d≥1 and q∈Rd, define Sd(q)i={qi/∑ℓ=1dqℓ,∑ℓ=1dqℓ>0,1/d,∑ℓ=1dqℓ≤0. Initialize the two regret vectors as q0=0∈Rm and r0=0∈Rn, and initialize the retained pair as z0=(Sm(0),Sn(0)). At iteration t≥0, query xt=Sm(qt),yt=Sn(rt),ut=Ayt,vt=C⊤xt. Define the instantaneous deviation gains and update the regret vectors by ait=uit−(xt)⊤ut,qit+1=max{qit+ait,0},bjt=vjt−(yt)⊤vt,rjt+1=max{rjt+bjt,0}. The retained pair is updated using the strategies queried at that iteration: zt+1={(xt,yt),ait≤ε for every i and bjt≤ε for every j,zt,otherwise. After the allotted T oracle calls, the output is (x¯,y¯)=zT. Thus, if no queried pair passes the test, the output remains the initial uniform pair.

The required accuracy is an ε-Nash equilibrium: x¯∈Δm,y¯∈Δn,(Ay¯)i≤x¯⊤Ay¯+ε,i=1,…,m,(C⊤x¯)j≤x¯⊤Cy¯+ε,j=1,…,n. These requirements hold for every realization, rather than only in expectation or almost surely.

Results

  • Regret Matching Potential · Lean proof

Two-player exact potential games with m,n≥1, B,ε>0, payoff matrices A,C, and an exact potential P∈[0,B]m×n: unilateral payoff differences equal potential differences; payoff levels are unrestricted. Each oracle call returns (Ay,C⊤x) exactly. The setup fixes zero-initialized, undiscounted simultaneous RM+; public inputs are m,n,ε, while B determines the horizon. Its saved certified pre-update profile (x,y)∈Δm×Δn satisfies maxi(Ay)i−x⊤Ay≤ε and maxj(C⊤x)j−x⊤Cy≤ε for every seed. Prior methods are compared on these games in paired-vector calls; alternating sweeps cost two calls.
