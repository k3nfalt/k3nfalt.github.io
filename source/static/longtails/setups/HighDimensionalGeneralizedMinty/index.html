High-Dimensional Minty Root Finding

We seek a zero of a continuous operator F:Rd→Rd in high dimension d. On each segment, the Lipschitz constant of F is at most L0+L1M whenever M bounds its Euclidean norm there, with L0>0 and L1≥0. A given starting point x0 lies within distance R≥0 of a zero z satisfying ⟨F(x),x−z⟩≥0 for all x∈Rd. The oracle returns F(x) at x. For tolerance ε>0, the goal is an output x¯∈Rd with integrable residual norm and E∥F(x¯)∥≤ε, where the expectation is over the algorithm’s randomness.

The residual minimization problem is minx∈Rd∥F(x)∥. The norm and inner product are Euclidean. The algorithm receives d∈N, L0, L1, R, ε, and x0∈Rd, and may query any point of Rd.

Our assumptions:

Assumption 1 (High-dimensional regime). With T=R(L0/ε+L1), the parameters satisfy T≥1024,L1R≥32log⁡(1+L1ε/L0),d≥8192(1+⌊(T/1024)2⌋)3.

Assumption 2 (Generalized Lipschitz continuity). The operator F is continuous. For every x,y∈Rd, let Mx,y=max0≤t≤1∥F(x+t(y−x))∥. Then ∥F(x)−F(y)∥≤(L0+L1Mx,y)∥x−y∥.

Assumption 3 (Minty zero). There exists z∈Rd such that F(z)=0, ∥x0−z∥≤R, and ⟨F(x),x−z⟩≥0for every x∈Rd.

After the allotted oracle calls, the residual norm ∥F(x¯)∥ must be integrable and satisfy E[∥F(x¯)∥]≤ε.

Results

  • Generalized Minty Lower Bound · Lean proof

An exact oracle returns the continuous field F:Rd→Rd. For all x,y, ∥F(x)−F(y)∥≤(L0+L1maxw∈[x,y]∥F(w)∥)∥x−y∥. A zero z satisfies ∥x0−z∥≤R and ⟨F(x),x−z⟩≥0 for all x. The target is an integrable residual with E∥F(X)∥≤ε. Here L0,ε>0, L1,R≥0, T≥1024, 32log⁡(1+L1ε/L0)≤L1R, and d≥8192(1+⌊(T/1024)2⌋)3.
